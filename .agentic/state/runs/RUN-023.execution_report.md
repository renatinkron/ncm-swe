## Key findings (recorded in paper §4.4 + TIMELINE + SOURCES)
- l0 source (model/uniform/unigram) barely affects stutter (~3 p.p., n=8 noise).
- Official sampling recipe (T=1.0, no top-k/top-p) halved dup_rate:
  0.2174 → 0.1095 — largest single factor measured.
- Decode penalties partial: freq=5 → 0.163; adj=(5,3,0.5) → 0.181.
- Exposure bias measured directly: CE 5.6481 self-gen vs 4.4124 held GT
  (+1.24 nats), concentrated at fine levels (l7 4.08 vs 2.00; l11 2.21 vs 0.65).
- Clean-context eval: v2 held_l11 = 0.65 without corruption (2.79 included
  training-protocol corruption).
- Literature: mode matches Gu et al. 2018 (NAT conditional-independence
  failure); independent WPS/MLX replication (960k windows) shows same stutter
  and CE↔generation inversion; per-level decaying corruption is the lever → v3.
