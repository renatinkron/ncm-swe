## Patterns

Five findings share one root cause — **measurement consistency is weaker than the pipeline's plumbing**: the unshared decode RNG (1), the train/eval featurization mismatch (2), arch selection on eval (3), baseline/head eval-set mismatch (4), and the contiguous-order subsample (5) all make the *comparisons* softer than the underlying feature/label alignment (which is well-guarded: mirrored `segs_of`, count asserts, `iter_rows` handling U+2028, shuffle control). The data path is careful; the evaluation path needs the same discipline.

## Verdict

The segmentation mirror, feature extraction alignment, labels slicing, and probe train/eval separation are all correct — no indexing, off-by-one, mask, or leakage bugs found, and the `levels[0]==1` shape assumption holds. The two issues that matter are both in `bench_l0cond.py`: the "paired" arms do not share decode sampling noise (so the headline adapter-vs-unigram cosine numbers are dominated by unshared draws at n=16), and the adapter's prefix featurization uses a different truncation length at eval than at train. Both are fixable in a few lines (per-arm seeding; `max_length=24` for prefixes). Minor findings are all methodology-rigor nits of the same family — worth fixing before these numbers are cited, none blocking.
